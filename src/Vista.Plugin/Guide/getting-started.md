# Getting started

Vista moves the game camera along paths you build, so you can film smooth shots for screenshots and videos. You fly a free camera, drop points along the way, and Vista joins them into a track.

Type `/vista` in chat to open the **Vista** window. The first time, Vista asks where to save your scenes.

## Your first shot

1. Open the mode menu at the top left of the **Vista** window and choose Edit. Your character stays put and the camera comes loose.
2. Fly to where the shot should start. `W` `A` `S` `D` move, `E` and `Q` go up and down, and the mouse looks around.
3. Press `Backtick` to add a point.
4. Fly on and add a few more points along the path you want.
5. Press `Space` to play the track. Press it again, or start flying, to stop.
6. In the Playlist on the right, click {icon:Plus} **Add to playlist** and choose Track 1.
7. Choose Live in the mode menu, then press `Space` to play the shot.

Choose Off to give the camera back to the game.
