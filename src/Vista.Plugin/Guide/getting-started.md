# Getting started

Vista moves the game camera for you, so you can film smooth shots for screenshots and videos. You fly a free camera around the scene and drop points along the way. Vista joins the points into a track, and the camera glides along that track when you play it.

To open the **Vista** window, type `/vista` in the chat box.

Vista doesn't save scenes. Your scene is lost when the plugin unloads or the game closes.

## Your first shot

1. Open the mode menu near the top left of the **Vista** window. It shows the current mode, which starts as Off. Choose Edit. Your character stays where they are, and the camera comes loose.
2. Fly the camera to where you want the shot to start. Use `W` `A` `S` `D` to move, `E` and `Q` to go up and down, and the mouse to look around.
3. Press `Backtick` to add a point where the camera is. The point appears in the list and in the world.
4. Fly on a little and press `Backtick` again. Add a few more points along the path you want the camera to take.
5. Press `Space` to play the track and watch the camera follow it. Press `Space` again to stop, or start flying.
6. In the Playlist on the right, click the plus button, **Add to playlist**, and choose Track 1.
7. Open the mode menu and choose Live. The shot waits at its first frame. Press `Space` to play it.

To give the camera back to the game, choose Off in the mode menu.

To learn what each mode does, see Modes.
