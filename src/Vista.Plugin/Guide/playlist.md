# Playlist

The playlist is the list of tracks that Live plays, in order from top to bottom. Each line in it is an entry. Live plays the first entry, then cuts straight to the next, and so on to the end.

The playlist is the column on the right of the **Vista** window. Click **Hide playlist** on the top row to close it, and **Show playlist** to bring it back.

You can only change the playlist in Edit mode. In the other modes you can see it, but it is greyed out.

## Adding tracks

There are three ways to add a track:

- Click **Add to playlist** at the top of the playlist and choose a track from the menu. It goes at the end.
- Drag a track's name from the Hierarchy onto the playlist. Drop it on an entry to put it in that entry's place and push the rest down, or drop it in the empty space below to put it at the end.
- Right-click a track's name in the Hierarchy and choose **Add to playlist**. It goes at the end.

You can add the same track more than once.

## Reordering and removing

To change the order, drag an entry onto another entry. It takes that entry's place. Drag it into the empty space below the list to move it to the end.

To remove an entry, hold the mouse over it and click **Remove from playlist**, the cross at the end of the line. This takes the entry out of the playlist and leaves the track itself alone. If you delete a track in the Hierarchy, its entries leave the playlist too.

## Repeats

The small cell near the end of each entry is **Repeats**. It sets how many times the entry plays its track before Live moves on.

- A number means the entry plays its track that many times. For a **Ping-pong** track, one time is there and back.
- A dash (—) means the entry follows the track and plays it once.
- ∞ means the entry follows a track that has **Loop** on, so it plays for ever.

To set it, click the cell, type a number from 1 to 99, and press `Enter` or click somewhere else. Press `Escape` to leave it as it was. To step it up or down, hold the mouse over the cell and turn the mouse wheel.

To make the entry follow its track again, clear the number or type 0. Turning the wheel down from 1 does the same.

## Entries that are never reached

An entry showing ∞ holds the playlist, so Live never gets past it. Every entry below it is greyed out, because it will never play. Give the ∞ entry a number of repeats to let Live move on.

Live also skips any entry whose track has no points. You can only switch to Live once at least one entry's track has points.

## Loop playlist

When Live reaches the end of the playlist, the camera holds on the last frame. Turn on **Loop playlist**, next to **Add to playlist**, to start again from the first entry instead.

## While Live plays

In Live, the heading of the playlist shows which entry is playing, as its number out of the total, followed by the track's name. The entry that is playing is highlighted.

## Characters not found

A red warning sign on an entry means its track watches or follows a character that Vista can't find nearby. The Watch Target and Follow Target pages explain what the camera does then.
