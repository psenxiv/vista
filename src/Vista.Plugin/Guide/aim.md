# Aim

Aim decides which way the camera points as it moves along a track. The points set where the camera goes, and the aim sets what it looks at. Roll and field of view come from the points.

Click {icon:Crosshairs} **Select aim** on the track row to choose:

| Aim | The camera looks |
|---|---|
| **Recorded aim** | Where each point looked, turning smoothly between them. New tracks start here. |
| **Direction of travel** | Ahead along the path, like a camera on a rail. |
| **Look At** | At one fixed spot. See [Look At](aim-look-at.md). |
| **Watch Target** | At a character. See [Watch Target](aim-watch-target.md). |
| **Follow Target** | From beside a character, travelling with them. See [Follow Target](aim-follow-target.md). |

{icon:PencilAlt} beside **Watch Target** or **Follow Target** in the menu reopens its window.

With **Direction of travel**, the menu also shows **Look ahead**: how far ahead along the path the camera looks, in yalms. The camera turns into corners before it reaches them. At 0 it faces straight along the path.

## Changing a point's aim

With **Recorded aim**, change a point's aim with **Pitch** and **Yaw** in the [Point window](tracks-point-window.md), or with the gizmo's rotate rings.

## Seeing where it turns

Press {key:ColourByTurnSpeed} to colour the edited track's path by how fast the camera turns: yellow, then red where it turns hardest.
