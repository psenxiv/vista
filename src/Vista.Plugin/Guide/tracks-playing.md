# Playing a track

Play a track to watch the shot it makes. The buttons sit at the bottom of the **Vista** window, to the left of the scrub bar.

| Button | Key | Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Play** / **Pause** | `Space` | Plays the track from where the scrub bar is, or pauses it. |
| **Restart** | `Ctrl + Space` | Plays the track from the beginning. |

If the track has already played to its end, **Play** starts it again from the beginning.

## Playing in Edit mode

In Edit mode, **Play** plays only the track you are editing, not the playlist. Vista stops drawing tracks and markers in the game world while it plays, so you see the shot as it will look. The buttons are greyed out until the track has a point.

Playing stops when you do something else. Flying the camera, changing the track or dragging the scrub bar all stop it. The camera stays where the track left it, and you fly on from there. Press **Play** again to carry on from that moment.

In Live mode the same buttons and keys play the playlist instead. The Live page explains how.

## The scrub bar

The scrub bar shows how far through the track you are, and the track's length, in seconds. Drag it to move through the track by hand. The camera shows that moment of the shot. When you let go, you can fly on from there.

## Direction and loop

The direction button is on the track row, and its icon is an arrow. Click **Select direction** to choose how the track plays:

- **Forward** plays from the first point to the last.
- **Reverse** plays from the last point to the first.
- **Ping-pong** plays from the first point to the last, then back to the first.

The button beside it is **Loop**. When it is on, its icon lights up and the track plays over and over until you pause it. Click it again to turn it off, and the track plays once and stops. Its tooltip says **Play once** while the loop is on.

You can change the direction and the loop in Edit mode only.
