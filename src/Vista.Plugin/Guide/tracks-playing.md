# Playing a track

Play a track to watch its shot. In Edit, playing shows only the track you are editing, and hides Vista's markers so you see the shot clean.

| Key | Button | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `Space` | {icon:Play} **Play** / {icon:Pause} **Pause** | Plays from the scrub bar, or stops. |
| `Ctrl + Space` | {icon:StepBackward} **Restart** | Plays from the beginning. |

Flying or changing the track stops playback, and you fly on from that frame.

Drag the scrub bar beside the buttons to move through the track by hand.

## Direction

{icon:ArrowRight} **Select direction** on the track row chooses how the track plays:

| Direction | Plays |
|---|---|
| **Forward** | First point to last. |
| **Reverse** | Last point to first. |
| **Ping-pong** | First to last, then back again. |

## Loop

Turn on {icon:Repeat} **Loop** to play the track over and over. Turn it off to play it once.

In Live, the playlist plays instead. See [Live](modes-live.md).
