# Watch Target

With **Watch Target**, the camera moves along the track as usual but keeps turning to look at a character. Use it to keep someone in frame while the camera flies past them.

## Choosing the character

Choosing **Watch Target** opens the **Watch Target** window.

1. Click **Choose a character**.
2. Pick a name from the characters nearby. Type in **Search** to find someone.
3. Click **Done**.

The aim button {icon:Crosshairs} turns the accent colour when the character is found, and red when not.

## Settings

| Setting | Does |
|---|---|
| **Aim height** | Where on the character the camera aims, in yalms above their feet. |
| **Smoothing** | How gently the camera turns to follow them. 0 stays exactly on them. |

A small crosshair on the character marks where the camera aims.
