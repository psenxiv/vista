# Presets

A preset is a track saved on its own, so you can reuse a shot, such as an orbit or a crane move, in any scene.

## Saving a preset

Right-click a track in the Hierarchy and choose **Save as preset**, then name it.

## Adding a preset

1. Fly the camera to where you want the shot.
2. Click {icon:Plus} **Add track or preset** at the top of the Hierarchy, then choose **Add preset**.
3. Pick the preset.

The preset appears as a new track on the ground under the camera, facing the way it was saved. Move or turn it with its anchor. See [Scenes and anchors](scenes.md).

{icon:Trash} **Delete** in the preset list deletes a preset.

{icon:FolderOpen} **Open presets folder** shows your preset files so you can share them.
