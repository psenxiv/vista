# Edit

Edit is where you build your shots. You fly a free camera around the scene, add points, and change your tracks. Most of the **Vista** window only works in Edit.

When you switch to Edit, your character stays locked in place. The game's zoom is turned off, and the movement keys fly the camera instead of your character.

## Flying the camera

- `W` `A` `S` `D` fly forward, left, back and right, in the direction the camera faces.
- `E` flies up and `Q` flies down.
- `Ctrl + Q` and `Ctrl + E` roll the camera left and right.
- The mouse looks around, the same way it turns the game camera.

To change how fast you fly, scroll the mouse wheel while the mouse is over the game. Scroll up to fly faster and down to fly slower. You can also drag the **Fly speed** slider on the top row of the **Vista** window. It has five steps, from 0.25x to 4x. Hold `Shift` to fly four times faster for as long as you hold it.

## Selecting in the world

Vista draws your tracks in the world while you edit. To select a point, click its marker. You can also click an anchor or a Look At point to select it.

If you click a point on another track, Vista switches to that track and selects the point. Click empty space to clear the selection. Dragging the mouse to look around doesn't count as a click, so it won't change what is selected.

For what you can do with a selected point, see The Point window.

## Undo and redo

Press `Ctrl + Z` to undo your last change and `Ctrl + Y` to redo it. The **Undo** and **Redo** buttons on the top row do the same. Undo and redo only work in Edit.

## Camera buttons on the top row

These appear on the top row only in Edit.

- **Level camera roll** sets the camera's roll back to level.
- **Camera** opens and closes the **Camera** window. See The Camera window.
