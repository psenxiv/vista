# Edit

Edit is where you build your shots. You fly a free camera, add points and change your tracks. Most of the **Vista** window only works in Edit.

## Flying

The camera flies like a plane, so the movement keys follow the way you're facing.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Fly forward, left, back and right |
| `E` / `Q` | Fly up / down |
| `Ctrl + Q` / `Ctrl + E` | Roll left / right |
| `Shift` | Fly faster while held |
| Mouse | Look around, even over the top |
| Mouse wheel | Change fly speed |

**Fly speed**, the slider on the top row, also sets the speed.

## Selecting

Click a point, an anchor or a Look At point in the world to select it. Clicking a point on another track switches to that track. Click empty space to clear the selection. `Ctrl` + click or `Shift` + click the points of the track you are editing to select several. See [Selecting several](tracks-selecting.md).

## The top row

| | Button | Does |
|---|---|---|
| {icon:Undo} | **Undo** | Undoes your last change. `Ctrl + Z` does the same. |
| {icon:Redo} | **Redo** | Redoes it. `Ctrl + Y` does the same. |
| {icon:ChartLine} | **Timing** | Opens the [timing graph](timing-graph.md). |
| {icon:RulerHorizontal} | **Level camera roll** | Levels the camera's roll. |
