# Selecting several

Select several tracks, points or playlist entries to move or change them together. You can select one kind at a time.

## Clicking

| Click | Does |
|---|---|
| Click | Selects only that row. |
| `Ctrl` + click | Adds the row to the selection, or removes it. |
| `Shift` + click | Adds every row from the last one you clicked to this one. |

These work in the Hierarchy, the points table and the Playlist, and on the points of the track you are editing in the world.

## Right-click menus

Right-click a selected row to act on the whole selection.

| Selected | Menu |
|---|---|
| Several tracks | **Add to playlist**, **Show**, **Hide**, **Delete** |
| Points | **Move to new track**, **Move to**, **Duplicate**, **Delete** |
| Several playlist entries | **Remove from playlist** |

## Dragging

Drag a selected row to move the whole selection. The rows move together and keep their order. Drag several tracks onto the Playlist to add them all.

Drag points onto a track in the Hierarchy to move them to the end of that track. Drop them on **New track**, the space below the tracks, to move them into a new track. The points stay where they are in the world.
