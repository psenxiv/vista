# Modes

Vista is always in one of four modes. The mode decides who moves the camera: you, the game, or Vista.

To switch mode, open the mode menu near the top left of the **Vista** window. It shows the name of the current mode. Choose the mode you want.

## Off

The game has its camera and Vista stays out of the way. Nothing is drawn in the world, and your character moves as usual. Vista starts in Off.

## View

The game has its camera, as in Off, and your character moves as usual. Vista draws the scene's tracks in the world so you can see where they run. You can look at them but not change them.

## Edit

You fly a free camera to build your tracks. Your character stays locked in place, and the game's zoom is turned off. Most of the **Vista** window only works in Edit. See Edit.

## Live

Vista plays the playlist through the camera. Your character stays locked, as in Edit. Live can only be chosen once the playlist has a track with points in it. See Live.

## Moving between modes

- Going from Off or View to Edit starts the free camera where the game camera is.
- Going from Live to Edit starts the free camera at the frame on screen, so you can fix what you see.
- Going to Off or View hands the camera back to the game, where it was before Vista took it.
- In Off or View, clicking **Play** goes straight to Live and starts playing.

You can also type `/vista release` in the chat box to switch to Off.
