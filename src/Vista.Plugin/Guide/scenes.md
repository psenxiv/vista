# Scenes and anchors

A scene is everything you are working on: your tracks, playlists and switchboard. It stays put when you change zone, and saves itself as you work. See [Saving and scenes](scenes-saving.md).

An anchor is a marker on the ground that other things hang off. Move or turn an anchor and everything attached moves with it.

| Anchor | Looks like | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| Scene anchor | A diamond with an arrow | Every track in the scene. |
| Track anchor | A ring with an arrow and the track's name | That track's points and Look At point. |

Anchors appear on the ground under the first point you add, so you never place them yourself.

## Moving an anchor

1. Select it: click it in the world, or click {icon:Anchor} beside the Scene heading or on the track's row in the Hierarchy.
2. Drag the gizmo's arrows to move it.
3. To turn it, click {icon:SyncAlt} **Rotate** in the **Point** window or press {key:GizmoToggle}, then drag the ring.

Hold `Alt` while you drag the gizmo to move the anchor alone, leaving everything attached where it is. Use it to turn a scene or track around a different spot.

**Move anchor to me** in the **Scene** menu puts the selected anchor where the camera is, with its points or on its own.
