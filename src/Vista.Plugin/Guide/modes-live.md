# Live

Live is for filming. Vista plays the playlist through the camera, one track after another, while you record. Your character stays locked in place, as in Edit.

You can only switch to Live once the playlist has a track with points in it. To add a track to the playlist, see Playlist.

## Playing

When you choose Live in the mode menu, the shot waits at the start of the playlist. Nothing moves until you play it.

- Press `Space` or click **Play** to play. Press `Space` again or click **Pause** to hold the current frame.
- Press `Ctrl + Space` or click **Restart** to play the playlist again from the beginning.
- Drag the scrub bar to move through the track that is playing now.

When the playlist reaches its end, the camera holds on the last frame. If **Loop playlist** is on, it starts again from the first entry instead.

While Vista is in Live, the word LIVE pulses in red on the top row of the **Vista** window.

## Hiding the game UI

Click **Hide game UI when Live** on the top row to hide the game's UI while a shot plays. You can turn it on before you go Live or while a shot is playing. Turn it off and the UI comes back at once.

To bring the game UI back, press `Escape`. This doesn't open the game's menu. To hide it again, press `Space` or click **Play**.

## Leaving Live

To stop, choose another mode in the mode menu. Choose Edit to fly on from the frame on screen, or Off to give the camera back to the game.

Vista also leaves Live on its own, and goes to Off, when you change zone or log out. This happens in Edit too.
