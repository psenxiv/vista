# Keys and easing

Change the shape of the line in the timing graph to make the camera ease into a move, stop at a point, or reach a point at an exact moment. You can only change it in Edit.

## Easing a leg

Click the line between two keys to select that leg, then choose its easing from the drop-down:

| Easing | The camera |
|---|---|
| **Smooth** | Changes speed gently through the points. New legs start here. |
| **Linear** | Moves at one steady speed. |
| **Ease in** | Starts from a stop. |
| **Ease out** | Slows to a stop. |
| **Ease in-out** | Starts and ends at a stop. |

## Keys

Click a key to select it, then choose how the line passes through it:

| | Button | The line |
|---|---|---|
| {icon:BezierCurve} | **Smooth** | Curves gently through the key. |
| {icon:Slash} | **Linear** | Runs straight in and out. |
| {icon:GripLines} | **Flat** | Stops for a moment at the key. |
| {icon:Trash} | **Remove hold** | Removes a hold, when its end dot is selected. |

## Dragging

- Drag a key left or right to change when the camera reaches it. The legs on either side adjust.
- Hold `Ctrl` as you drag to move every later key with it, making the track longer or shorter.
- Drag a hold's end dot to change the hold's length.
- Drag a selected key's handles to make the line steeper or flatter there.

Right-click a key to **Break handles**, so each side moves on its own, or **Unify handles** again.
