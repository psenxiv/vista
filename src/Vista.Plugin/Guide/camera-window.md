# The Camera window

The **Camera** window shows where the camera you fly in Edit mode is, and lets you type exact numbers for it. It is useful when you want the camera perfectly level, or at a precise height or field of view.

To open it, click **Camera** on the top row of the **Vista** window. Click it again to close it. The button and the window only show in Edit mode.

## What it shows

The window is laid out like the **Point** window, with three rows. Hold the mouse over a field to see its name.

- **Position**: **X**, **Y** and **Z**, where the camera is in the world.
- **Rotation**: **Pitch**, **Yaw** and **Roll**, in degrees. Pitch tilts the camera up or down, yaw turns it left or right, and roll tilts it to one side.
- **FoV**: the field of view, in degrees. A smaller number zooms in.

The numbers change as you fly. To change one yourself, drag the field left or right, or double-click it and type a number. The camera moves as you change it.

These changes move the camera, not your track, so `Ctrl + Z` doesn't undo them.

The buttons along the top are greyed out here. They only work in the **Point** window.

## Level roll and field of view

The button at the start of the Rotation row is **Level roll**. It sets the roll back to zero, so the horizon is flat.

The button at the start of the FoV row is **Reset to the game's field of view**. It puts the field of view back to what the game was using before Vista took the camera.

You don't need the window open to level the camera. The **Level camera roll** button on the top row of the **Vista** window does the same as **Level roll**. It shows in Edit mode, beside the **Camera** button.
