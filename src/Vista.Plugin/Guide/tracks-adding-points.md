# Adding points

Fly the camera to where you want a point, aim it, then add the point. It records the camera as it is now.

| Key | Menu item | Does |
|---|---|---|
| {key:AddToEnd} | **Add to end** | Adds a point after the last one. |
| {key:AddAfterSelected} | **Add after selected** | Adds a point after the selected one. |
| {key:OverwriteSelected} | **Overwrite selected** | Replaces the selected point with the camera, keeping its timing. |

{icon:Plus} **Add point** on the track row adds to the end, and {icon:CaretDown} **More ways to add** beside it has all three.

## The points table

The table in the middle of the **Vista** window lists the track's points. The number matches the one shown on the point in the world.

- Click a row to select the point. The [Point window](tracks-point-window.md) opens.
- Double-click a row's number to move the camera to that point.
- Press {key:DeleteSelectedPoints} to delete the selected points.

The timing columns are covered in [Legs and holds](timing-legs-and-holds.md).

## Duplicating points

Duplicate makes an exact copy of a point straight after it.

Right-click a point's row and choose **Duplicate**, or choose **Duplicate point** in the **Edit** menu. With several points selected, each one gets its own copy.
