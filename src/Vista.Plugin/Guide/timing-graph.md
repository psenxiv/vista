# The timing graph

The timing graph shows how the camera moves along the track over time. Click {icon:ChartLine} **Timing** on the top row to open it.

## Reading it

Time runs left to right, in seconds. Distance along the track runs bottom to top, in yalms. Where the line is steep the camera moves fast, and where it is flat the camera is still.

- Each numbered circle is a point's key: where the camera reaches that point.
- A hold is a flat stretch ending in a small dot.
- The vertical line is the current moment in the track.

Hover the line to see the time, distance and speed there.

## Moving through time

Click or drag in the strip along the bottom to move the camera to that moment.

## Zooming

Scroll over the graph to zoom in and out around the mouse. When zoomed in, drag empty space to move sideways. {icon:Expand} **Show the whole track** zooms back out.

To change the line, see [Keys and easing](timing-keys.md).
